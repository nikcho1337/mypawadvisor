### What the market rewards and punishes

Rewards: a sample reading visible in the photos, an explicit page count, a stated turnaround ("within 24 hours", "same hour"), "written by a real astrologer, not AI", breed-aware wording, owner-and-pet bond content, the pet's photo inside the design, a proof step, a framed option, memorial and Gotcha Day framings, strike-through sale pricing.

Punishes: vague or generic text, obvious AI boilerplate, PNG-only deliverables, zero reviews with no sample, unclear deliverable, slow or unstated delivery.

### Pricing

Digital pet readings cluster at $9.99 to $22.47 list with 30 to 50% strike-through sales landing at $7.49 to $11.24. Your 6.99 EUR sits in the bottom fifth of the band and below every bestselling human chart print. The $4.40 listing has only 3 reviews, so price is not what is holding sales back; visibility and trust are.

## 3. Ranked upgrades to the current product

Effort: S = under a day, M = a few days, L = a week or more.

### Add to the deliverable

1. **Placement table and methodology box** (S, high). A compact table of Sun, Moon, Rising, Mercury, Venus, Mars with degrees and houses, the house system, and a plain note that an unknown birth time gives a solar chart. You already compute all of it. Buyers of astrology products check the raw placements first, and competitors lead with them.
2. **Gotcha Day chart for pets with unknown birthdays** (S to M, very high). Accept the adoption date as the chart moment and label it a Gotcha Day or Homecoming chart. A large share of adopted pets have no confirmed birthday, and Gotcha Day products have hundreds of reviews. Label it clearly so purists are not misled.
3. **Owner and pet compatibility page** (M, high). Owner birth date in, a one-page synastry-lite out: element harmony, Sun/Moon/Venus contacts, three bond tips. The only quoted pet-chart review in the niche praises exactly this, and off-Etsy sellers charge A$140 for it. Natural Valentine's and Mother's Day product.
4. **Breed-flavoured variant banks** (M, medium-high). Blend breed group (herding, toy, sighthound; Siamese, Maine Coon) into the "operating style" and "love and play" paragraphs. Start with about ten groups. Reviews in this niche praise breed accuracy specifically.
5. **A 6 to 8 page edition, not 20** (M, medium). Two pages looks thin against listings advertising 11, 20 and 60 pages, but long human readings draw "generic" complaints. Add per-planet pages and a "how to read your pet's chart" page, keeping every paragraph placement-specific.
6. **Memorial edition** (S, medium-high). "In loving memory" theme, years instead of a birthdate, a softer wording bank, optional "the sky the night you said goodbye". Memorial maps are the best-selling adjacent product.
7. **Year-ahead page** (M, medium, seasonal). Slow transits only (Jupiter and Saturn to the natal Sun and Moon) so the engine stays deterministic. Sells in December and January.
8. **Night-sky panel** (M to L, strong differentiator). The real star field at the birth moment behind or beside the wheel. Only worth it if the stars and planets are accurate; accuracy is what the star-map market competes on.
9. **Multi-format export** (S, medium). A4, US Letter, 8x10, 11x14 and a 5x7 card in one ZIP. Pre-empts "does not fit my frame".
10. **Proof step** (S, medium). A low-resolution preview for approval within 24 hours on Etsy; the website already shows the wheel before payment.

### Add to the listing and marketing

11. **Ten photos plus a 5 to 15 second silent video** (S, high). Framed lifestyle mockup, close-up of legible placements, a page-2 excerpt, a "what you receive" infographic (pages, formats, delivery time), order form to PDF, the three themes side by side, the photo medallion, a gift mockup.
12. **Put "sample in photos", the page count and "delivered within 24h" in the title and first line** (S, high).
13. **A "hand-written interpretations, real ephemeris, no AI text" trust block** (S, medium-high). Your variant-bank architecture is a real differentiator; say it.
14. **Re-anchor the price** (S, medium). List at 12.99 to 14.99 EUR with a 40 to 45% launch sale landing at 7.49 to 8.49 EUR, matching the strike-through norm. Keep 6.99 only for a chart-only tier. Run genuine sale windows (EU rules require showing the lowest price of the prior 30 days).
15. **One listing per search cluster** (S, medium-high). Separate listings for dog birth chart, cat birth chart, Gotcha Day chart, memorial chart, owner-pet compatibility and framed print. Etsy has distinct market pages for each phrase; one listing cannot rank for all of them.
16. **All 13 tags and the occasion attributes** (S). Pet birth chart, dog natal chart, cat astrology gift, pet zodiac print, gotcha day gift, pet memorial gift, dog mom gift, astrology lover gift, personalized pet gift, custom star chart, new puppy gift, kitten gift. Occasions: Christmas, Valentine's, birthday, pet adoption.
17. **Review bootstrap** (S). Use the cheap chart-only tier to collect the first ten reviews, follow each order with a free one-paragraph "lucky month" note and a review request, and offer a free follow-up question like the psychic sellers do.
18. **Pinterest** (S). Human birth-chart prints are heavily pinned. Pin each theme plus a Gotcha Day and a memorial version; the poster script in `scripts/pinterest/` is ready once Pinterest approves standard access.

### New product variants

19. **Framed print via Prodigi** (M, high for Q4). Cost base about 10 to 19 GBP plus shipping; framed personalised charts sell at $35 to $53, and the 740-review framed memorial map is $35. Price 39 to 49 EUR for A4 in a classic frame and set a Christmas order deadline around December 10 to 12 for EU and UK.
20. **Owner and pet compatibility reading as its own listing** (M), 9.99 to 12.99 EUR, with Valentine's, Galentine's and Mother's Day hooks.
21. **Pet memorial chart** (S), see item 6.
22. **Multi-pet bundle** (S). Two-pet and three-pet bundles at a discount; apps already price per pet.
23. **Gotcha Day certificate plus chart bundle** (S). The $2.39 certificate with 4,000 reviews proves the framing; yours is the premium version.

### Do not bother

- A personalized hardcover book ($94 to $97, one review after a long time on sale).
- Subscription horoscopes or an app (competitors show no visible traction).
- Plain custom star maps as a standalone product (you would face shops with 36,000 reviews); use the sky only as a panel on the chart.
- 50 to 60 page reports (long readings draw "vague" complaints).
- Chinese zodiac, birth flower or birthstone add-ons for pets (no demand found).
- Chart mugs, ornaments or metal prints until the framed print proves out.
- Cutting the price below 6.99 EUR.
- Etsy Ads before roughly ten reviews.

## 4. Product ideas from the website's own keyword data

The site's search demand (9,324 impressions in 28 days) is almost entirely commercial research for products you do not make: automatic litter boxes (3,301 impressions), pet insurance comparison (1,449), GPS trackers (about 760), FURminator tools (about 800 across pages), breed dog food and cat food. That demand monetises through affiliate clicks, not through a product you could sell. The honest ranking of custom-product ideas is:

| Idea | Fit with the keyword data | Why it ranks where it does |
|---|---|---|
| Gotcha Day / birth certificate plus natal chart bundle | Indirect | Reuses the engine, matches a proven Etsy format, and gives the website's dog-owner audience a gift to buy. Best next product. |
| Owner and pet compatibility reading | Indirect | Same engine, strongest seasonal hook, under-served on Etsy. |
| Pet memorial chart | Indirect | Largest proven adjacent market; low effort. |
| Personalized breed feeding chart or portion poster (pet name, breed, weight) | Direct (breed dog food queries) | Plausible printable, but buyers of "best dog food for labs" want a recommendation, not a poster; low willingness to pay. |
| Litter box or GPS tracker "match quiz" | Direct (litter box and tracker queries) | Valuable, but as a free tool that lifts affiliate clicks on the pages that already rank, not as a paid product. |
| Pet health record or new-puppy binder printable | Weak | Commodity on Etsy at $3 to $8 with thousands of competitors. |

So: build the natal line extensions for income, and treat the keyword demand as an affiliate-conversion problem (titles, comparison tables, buy buttons), which is what this session's site changes addressed.
